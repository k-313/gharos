Security & Privacy

Privacy-first by design. Human-in-the-loop by default.

Household data is intimate. GharOS enforces strict boundaries: zero autonomous writes without your explicit approval.

🛡️
Mandatory Consent

User Approval Required

GharOS never books reminders, executes transfers, or informs roommates autonomously. Every action requires a verified 1-tap confirmation.

🔒
Least Privilege

Minimum Permissions

Scoped read-only filters target only specific utility providers and invoices. Personal emails, medical records, and chats remain inaccessible.

📜
Verifiable Trail

Immutable Action History

Every parsed statement, calendar entry, and ledger division is logged with tamper-evident timestamps viewable on demand via chat (`/audit`).

🗑️
Instant Purge

Data Deletion Controls

Send "Forget My Data" at any moment to trigger cryptographic erasure of all parsed bills, cached tokens, and household records.
